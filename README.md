# 🚀 Retail Inventory & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Retail Inventory & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms inventory and supply-chain data into meaningful visual insights, helping analyze **inventory levels, sales, logistics, warehouse operations, supplier performance, products, stockouts, lost sales, and reorder requirements**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- 📦 Monitor inventory and stock levels
- 📈 Analyze sales performance and trends
- 🚚 Track inventory received and logistics activity
- 🏭 Analyze warehouse operations and stock movement
- 👥 Analyze supplier contribution
- 🛍️ Evaluate product performance
- ⚠️ Identify stockout and lost-sales situations
- 🔄 Identify products below reorder levels
- 📊 Build interactive KPIs and visualizations
- 💡 Support data-driven business decisions

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

The dashboard contains **7 interactive pages**:

### 🏠 1. Executive Overview

Provides an overall summary of inventory and supply-chain performance.

**Key areas:**
- Total Units Sold
- Total Units Received
- Closing Stock
- Lost Sales
- Stockout Rate
- Products Below Reorder

---

### 📦 2. Inventory Analysis

Analyzes inventory availability and stock requirements.

**Key areas:**
- Stock Level Trends
- Stock by Category
- Top Products by Stock
- Products Below Reorder
- Safety Stock
- Stockout Rate

---

### 📈 3. Sales Analysis

Provides insights into sales performance.

**Key areas:**
- Sales Trends
- Sales by Category
- Top Products by Sales
- Sales Share by Category
- Average Weekly Sales
- Products Sold

---

### 🚚 4. Logistics Analysis

Analyzes inventory received and supplier-related logistics activity.

**Key areas:**
- Received Units Trend
- Received Units by Category
- Received Units by Supplier
- Received Units vs Lost Sales
- Supplier Count
- Stores Supplied

---

### 🏭 5. Warehouse Analysis

Analyzes stock movement and warehouse operations.

**Key areas:**
- Opening vs Closing Stock
- Stock Movement by Category
- Spoilage by Category
- Stock Distribution by Store
- Total Spoilage
- Stockout Rate

---

### 👥 6. Supplier Analysis

Analyzes supplier contribution and inventory received.

**Key areas:**
- Received Units by Supplier
- Received Units by Category
- Supplier Contribution
- Received Units Trend
- Total Suppliers
- Stores Supplied

---

### 🛍️ 7. Product Analysis

Provides product-level insights into sales and inventory.

**Key areas:**
- Top Products by Sales
- Stock by Category
- Products Below Reorder
- Product Sales Trend
- Total Products
- Products Sold
